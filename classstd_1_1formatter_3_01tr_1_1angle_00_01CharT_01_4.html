Detailed Description

template<typename CharT>
class std::formatter< tr::angle, CharT >

Angle formatter.

Angles may be output in radians, degrees, or turns, indicated by a leading r/d/t in the format specification. Afterwards, a floating-point format specification may be used to indicate the numeric format.

Example format output: std::format("{:d.3f}", tr::degs(1.23456f)) -> 1.235deg.
