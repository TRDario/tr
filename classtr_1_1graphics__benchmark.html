Detailed Description

Graphics benchmark.

The graphics benchmark mostly has the same usage pattern as the regular benchmark, with .start() and .stop() methods delineating the benchmarked region of code, however it has an additional .fetch() method that much be called to make the measurement accessable to the CPU and add it to the measurement deque.
